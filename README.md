## About

IDEA Club is a student community built around **curiosity, ambition, and action**. We explore real-world problems, collaborate across disciplines, learn the skills we need along the way, and turn ideas into research, projects, innovations, and opportunities.

> You do not need to be an AI expert or brilliant coder to belong here. Come as you are. Bring your idea, your curiosity, and your vision.

## What We're Building

**Explore. Research. Build. Grow.**

- 🔍 **Explore** — Find meaningful problems and discover where AI can make a difference.
- 🤝 **Collaborate** — Work with students from different disciplines, backgrounds, and skill levels.
- 📚 **Research** — Turn questions and ideas into research, publications, and new knowledge.
- 🚀 **Build** — Develop solutions, prototypes, startups, innovations, and opportunities.

Not an expert? That's okay — skills are built along the way. What matters is your zeal, ambition, curiosity, and desire to contribute.
